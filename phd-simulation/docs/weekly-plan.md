## First month: concrete targets
Week 1: run the toy, explain each term, distinguish feasible optimum from penalized optimum.
Week 2: derive one-qubit probabilities and create a notation sheet; read two verified sources.
Week 3: implement and test bit ordering and measurement conventions; give a five-minute explanation.
Week 4: write a two-page proposal, map at least six relevant sources and decide whether the topic is narrow enough.

## Daily routine example
08:30 plan and review yesterday; 09:00–11:00 uninterrupted derivation/coding; 11:00 seminar or admin; 11:30 lunch; 12:00–14:00 experiment or reading; 14:00 collaboration/coursework; 15:00 write and commit; 16:30 finish.
For an ordinary full week, aim for roughly 14 hours implementation/experiments, 8 reading/coursework, 7 writing/analysis, 4 meetings and 4.5 admin/rework. Adjust to the phase.
There is no requirement to produce a novel result every day.
