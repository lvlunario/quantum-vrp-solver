# Fictional collaboration network
Every assignment below is a storytelling role, NOT an existing consortium or a claim about any institution's participation in this project.
No real person is portrayed as having spoken to Leo. Meetings use roles such as “Nordic methods colleague.”

| Setting | Imagined research role | Contact cadence within the story |
|---|---|---|
| Simula, Norway | Supervisor, software verification and research design | Weekly 30–45 minute supervision; fortnightly lab meeting |
| Aalto University, Finland | Reproducibility and hybrid computing | Monthly |
| RIKEN, Japan | Circuit execution and noise assumptions | Every 6–8 weeks; morning Oslo call |
| DTU, Denmark | Power-system modeling critique | Monthly during model design |
| Barcelona Supercomputing Center, Spain | Classical baselines and compute budgeting | At benchmark milestones |
| ETH Zurich, Switzerland | Optimization and statistical critique | Quarterly |
| Inria, France | Software testing and fault models | Monthly during verification work |
| TU Delft, Netherlands | Circuit mapping and measurement interpretation | At simulation milestones |
| UC San Diego, United States | Systems validation and energy case-study review | Every 6–8 weeks; occasional late Oslo call |
| University of Padua, Italy | Independent reproduction and artifact review | During reproduction and paper revision |

Use two or three active collaborators in any given month, not nine weekly meetings.
One simulated Nordic visit in spring and one simulated workshop trip in summer are sufficient; most collaboration is asynchronous.
Travel does not imply tickets, registrations, access to facilities or funds were obtained.
Use Europe/Oslo, Asia/Tokyo and America/Los_Angeles timezone conversions for actual story dates, including daylight-saving changes.
Compensate for a late US call with a later start. Do not schedule all time zones into a single convenient but impossible call.
