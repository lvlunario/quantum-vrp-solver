# Kickoff talk source — fictional lab, 8 minutes
## Slide 1 — Can we trust the answer?
Research question: How do we detect quantum optimization outputs that violate the engineering problem?
Speaker note: explain connection to systems verification and energy operations.
## Slide 2 — Three loads, five units of supply
Demands 2,3,4; priorities 3,5,6. Serving loads 0 and 1 is the constrained optimum.
Speaker note: have the audience enumerate feasible sets before showing an algorithm.
## Slide 3 — The deceptive objective
At penalty 0.1, all loads on has energy 1.6 but violates capacity. Lower objective does not imply acceptable operation.
## Slide 4 — Evidence collected today
Exact enumeration, ideal three-qubit statevector sweep, stored probability vectors and three independent checks.
Speaker note: no hardware run, no speedup, no general benchmark yet.
## Slide 5 — Proposed contribution
Trace requirements through formulation, circuit and decoding; design independent and metamorphic checks.
## Slide 6 — Questions for the group
Is the fault model realistic? Is the research gap already solved? Which classical baseline would invalidate a weak comparison?
Speaker note: next milestone is a checked encoding and literature map, not a claimed quantum advantage.
