## 09:15–10:30 — Mathematical coursework: what does “feasible” mean?

The six-variable toy has three load bits and three slack bits. Physical feasibility asks whether selected demand is at most capacity five. Encoding consistency additionally asks whether the selected slack exactly fills the unused capacity. These events are different. A load choice can be safe while its auxiliary bits encode the wrong residual.

I derived three readouts separately: expected penalized energy, probability of physical feasibility, and probability of encoding consistency. Because slack is nonnegative, the encoding event is a subset of the physical event for this fixture. Marginalizing over all eight slack encodings should preserve the load probabilities. This becomes an executable invariant rather than a naming preference.
