## Full-text reading

I read the full text of Farhi, Goldstone and Gutmann's original QAOA paper (arXiv:1411.4028), recording equation-level notes rather than relying on the abstract. Equations (2)–(6) give the conventions that matter immediately: a diagonal cost unitary (\exp(-i\gamma C)), a mixer generated by (B=\sum_jX_j), a uniform initial state, and alternating cost-then-mixer layers. Equation (7) also exposed a potential sign error. The paper maximizes (C); our toy is written as an energy (E) to minimize. The project decision is to retain (C=E), evolve with (\exp(-i\gamma E)), and minimize expected energy. Any future report must state that convention explicitly.

I then checked the current IBM Quantum `RXGate` reference. Qiskit defines `RX(theta)` as (\exp(-i\theta X/2)), so the paper's (\exp(-i\beta X)) requires `RX(2*beta)`. That factor is now an acceptance test. A circuit that uses `RX(beta)` may look plausible and remain unitary while implementing the wrong variational family.

The reading also tightened the claim boundary. The foundational paper discusses angle selection as an important difficulty and does not establish generic quantum advantage. Its finite-depth analytic examples are not evidence for this microgrid encoding. The project will therefore verify the implementation before asking whether the variational state improves any energy metric.
