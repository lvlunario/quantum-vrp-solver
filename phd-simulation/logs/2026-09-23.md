## 09:15–11:30 — Hand enumeration before more software
The morning work product is an eight-row table of every service decision. The feasible optimum serves the first two loads and has unserved priority six. Three decisions overload capacity. For each one, you solve the inequality requiring its penalized score to exceed six. The resulting thresholds are three quarters, one, and three eighths. Their maximum is one, and the inequality must be strict.

This exposes the day's useful failure: at penalty one, serving the first and third loads gives demand six, unserved priority five, and penalty one. Its total is six, exactly the same score as the best feasible schedule. The existing `argmin` report can return the feasible schedule because of its enumeration order. That does not make the other minimizer disappear. The old calculation need not be numerically wrong for its interpretation to be incomplete.
