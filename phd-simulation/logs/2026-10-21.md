## 12:15–13:15 — SIMULATED NARRATIVE: testing-method discussion

A fictional France software-testing role challenged the habit of calling every

The decision was to organize R06 as a fault-to-oracle matrix rather than a list

occupy different layers and cannot be collapsed into one generic “quantum test.”
